# Roadmap

Roughly in the order they itch:

- **Pocket events webhook** — let sibling apps push "something happened" instead of being polled every 30 minutes.
- **HealthKit through the Pocket Homelab app** — steps, sleep and workouts as a source.
- **A route map** on the day from Dawarich points, with the photos placed on it.
- **Nextcloud calendar** — the day's events from CalDAV.
- **Pocket Split and Pocket Inventory** — shared expenses and things bought that day.
- **Week view** — seven days side by side with a weekly line.
- **Printable year book** — the year recap and every written day as a PDF.
- **Mood ↔ facts insights** — do days with more walking, sun or cooking feel better?
